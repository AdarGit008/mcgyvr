## Five scouts, and what they overturned

Reconnaissance across vLLM's source, the `local-ai` repo, this repo, community
prior art, and a read-only scan of both hosts. **Five things this session
believed and then measured to be false**, each corrected by a rig:

- AWQ-Marlin gated at sm_80 with a Turing fallback — **the floor is 75**
  (`marlin_utils.py:64`) and both rigs resolve `MarlinLinearKernel`. The claim
  traces to vLLM #1282, from 2023, and is still repeated in 2026 write-ups.
- `--enforce-eager` mandatory on compute capability 7.5 — **no capability gate
  on graph capture exists** in 0.26.0 and vLLM's own feature table lists CUDA
  graph as supported on Turing.
- Concurrency bounded by `max_num_seqs x max_model_len <= KV tokens` — **srv1 ran
  64** on a server printing `Maximum concurrency: 16.00x`. KV is allocated per
  token actually used.
- `--performance-mode throughput` winning because of its name — it **lost** to
  plain no-eager, 2,012 against 2,601.
- Speculative decoding raising throughput — it **lowers** it at every
  concurrency measured, on both rigs.

`local-ai` had already recorded the srv1 collapse twice and attributed it to
"AWQ on Turing"; that attribution is half right and the half that is wrong
matters — see the mechanism below.

## The mechanism, isolated

`Qwen2.5-Coder` sets `tie_word_embeddings: true`, so **lm_head is the embedding
and AWQ never quantizes it**: an unquantized fp16 `[151936 x hidden]` matmul
through cuBLAS, every decode step, with M = running sequences. cuBLAS dispatches
a GEMV at M=1 and a tiled GEMM at M>=2. The 1660 SUPER is TU116, the one Turing
die with no tensor cores.

Measured with the model out of the picture — that GEMM alone, same container,
both cards:

| M | srv1 fp16 | srv1 fp32 | srv2 fp16 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1.77 ms | 2.95 | 1.39 |
| 2 | **50.83** | 2.98 | 1.39 |
| 32 | 50.85 | 11.63 | 1.46 |

**Excess 49.06 ms against the 49.79 ms the live run shows — 1.5%.** Controls
pass in both directions: fp32 on the same card does not cliff, fp16 on the other
card does not cliff. The 3B's penalty scales exactly with hidden size
(65.6 / 49.1 = 1.336 against 2048/1536 = 1.333).

**Why "AWQ on Turing" is the wrong name for it.** The AWQ body goes through
Marlin's own `mma` kernels and is fine. Only the *unquantized* head hits cuBLAS.
Dropping quantization would put every matmul on the 50 ms path and be worse.

## The sweep

140 cells, 20 axes, both rigs, `tools/bench/serving/sweep.py` and
`records/evidence/2026-08-24-config-sweep/`.

| rig | model | baseline | best | gain |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| srv1 | 1.5B | 164.3 | **294.7** | 1.79x |
| srv2 | 1.5B | 530.1 | **6,445.1** | **12.16x** |
| srv2 | Qwen3-4B | 370.0 | 2,349.4 | 6.35x |
| srv2 | 7B | 507.2 | 1,674.3 | 3.30x |

- **srv1 responds to one axis of twenty.** 25 cells inside a 2.8% band; only
  concurrency moves it, to ~295, where five configurations agree within 0.4%
  and four context lengths agree to four significant figures.
- **srv1's one lever is gated shut.** fp8 KV produced srv2's best cell — it
  doubles the sequences that fit rather than speeding a kernel up — and srv1
  refuses all three fp8 variants by compute capability.
- **An axis can be inert in one regime and decisive in another.** fp8 KV reads
  558 at n=16 (indistinguishable from baseline) and 6,445 at n=256.
- **The 7B has no step at all** — its lm_head is untied, therefore quantized,
  therefore on Marlin. It is *cheaper* per step at batch 8 than at batch 1.
- **The decisive test could not be run.** It needed an untied model on srv1; the
  7B OOMs allocating 518 MiB with 151.88 MiB free, in `auto_awq.py:533`, before
  KV is considered. srv1 caches no untied model it can hold.

The client was falsified as a bottleneck: max/mean request latency *falls*
toward 1.00 as concurrency rises (1.15 at n=32, 1.02 at n=256).

## Kept

- **A pinned constant is a blind spot with a good reason attached.**
  `calibrate.py`'s serve dict is hardcoded so the campaign's columns stay
  reproducible, and the cost was that 11 hours of measurement never varied a
  flag worth 5x. Both instruments now exist, and neither replaces the other.
- **A refusal is a result.** 36 of 140 cells refused, and the refusals are the
  clearest statement of what srv1 is: bf16 and every fp8 KV dtype, gated by
  silicon.
- **Two of this session's own defects produced false facts.** A shell-quoting
  bug word-split `--speculative-config`'s JSON and recorded three untested cells
  per rig as REFUSED; a 25-line log tail lost the srv1 7B root cause until it
  was re-run by hand. Both are in `sweep.py`'s docstring, and the stage-1
  records carrying the false refusals are kept as written.
