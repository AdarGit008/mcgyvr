## new_findings
- Root cause of the 11.3pp vs 8.2pp MDE disagreement (#263 item 3) is now settled by inspection: both figures come from the same function with the same psi_draw=0.659 and differ only in n. 8.2pp = detectable_delta(800, 0.659) — tools/bench/responsiveness.py:88-89 sizes against COMPLETE_CELLS = 400 problems x 2 arms = 800, citing only ADR-0021's FIRST 2026-08-12 amendment. 11.3pp = detectable_delta(426, 0.659) — tools/bench/redundancy.py's denominator section implements ADR-0021's SECOND 2026-08-12 amendment (only the bench half is ever swept: 400 authored -> 213 bench -> 426 cells). Both reproduce exactly by running mde.py. responsiveness.py's 800-cell denominator is the superseded one; the fix is small and #263's acceptance box 2 ('each states the unit it is the MDE for') is dischargeable without any sweep.
- #263 item 2 upgraded from carried-unverified to CONFIRMED by re-derivation: tools/bench/redundancy.py:245 prints a Pearson 95% CI as 1.96/sqrt(n) with n = 18,090 within-arm pairs built from only 270 cells (135 problems per arm; C(135,2)x2 = 18,090). Treating problems as the independent unit the interval is sqrt(18090/135) ~ 11.6x too narrow (~12x as the audit said); treating cells, ~8.2x. Live run prints r = -0.0126, CI +-0.0146. The published conclusion (no similarity-divergence correlation) survives — r is tiny under any honest interval — but the interval itself is invalid.
- Adopting mid-p (#271, wall 6 -> 5) flips NO committed verdict today: the twelve committed norule tier x arm x stratum contrasts have m in {1, 2, 3, 6, 7, 22} — none at exactly m=5 — and the ablation contrasts run m = 6-19. Mid-p at m=4 all-one-way gives p = 0.0625 > 0.05, so the m in {1,2,3} strata are not rescued either. The decision's value is prospective sizing only. Did not find any committed contrast recorded at m=5 (searched resolution output for both tiers, both ablation reports, and grep over docs/ and records/sessions/).
- The 'seven strata that cannot resolve at any n' claim (docs/identity-surface-2026-08-16.md section 4, repeated in ADR-0019's 2026-08-17 amendment) does not reproduce. Literally-any-n is false for every stratum: with psi > 0, required_n(delta=psi, psi) is finite — 58 to 272 cells across the twelve strata. At proportional 400-problem scaling the honest count is SIX strata still unresolvable (all four function_implementation+scaffold cells: psi 0.029, need ~272 cells, get 53; bench-ts bug_fix+scaffold at both tiers: need 154/232, get 92), plus TWO only marginally resolvable (py bug_fix+scaffold at both tiers: MDE 9.8pp vs psi 10.2pp, and 10.9pp vs 11.9pp — detectable only if nearly every discordant pair moves one way). Neither reading yields seven.
- The m>=6 wall constant is defined in at least three places at tip — tools/power/mde.py:45 (MIN_DISCORDANT=6), tools/bench/ablation_report.py:108 (DISCORDANT_WALL=6), tools/bench/resolution.py:80 (WALL=6; responsive.py imports resolution's) — and significance itself is implemented at least twice (mde.exact_p and ablation_report.sign_test compute the same exact binomial independently; two deliberate fisher copies besides). This is the exact surface #271's acceptance item 'one significance implementation, shared' names.
- The ablation m and p values published by ablation_report.py are computed over pooled draws that include the greedy one (defect #263 item 1), so any m=5-vs-6 or significance re-read under #271 would be re-reading numbers already contaminated by #263 item 1 — the two issues intersect on the same rows.
- resolution.py figures carry two honesty caveats for any plan that quotes them: the tool is untested (only test contact is a static grep for 'mode.banner(' in tests/test_bench_rounds.py:551-556) and label-keyed (--tier is caller-supplied free text, resolution.py:151; docstring lines 37-42 concede two runs agreeing on labels but differing in the bar would be laid side by side); and the 1.5B contrast's stock run predates #231's product pin ('round: none recorded'), while the 7B pair is pinned to r1-commissioning.

## plan_input
- Resolution ground truth, today (257 problems/arm, lever norule, greedy, gate-scored; from tools/bench/resolution.py at tip): 1 of 12 tier x arm x stratum cells can resolve anything at all — 1.5B/bench-ts/function_implementation: n=164, psi=0.134, m=22, MDE 8.5pp. All eleven others are below the m>=6 wall or below 80% power: 1.5B py bug_fix+scaffold (n=59, psi .102, m=6), 1.5B py f_i (164, .043, 7), 1.5B py f_i+scaffold (34, .029, 1), 1.5B ts bug_fix (59, .051, 3), 1.5B ts f_i+scaffold (34, .029, 1), 7B py bug_fix (59, .119, 7), 7B py f_i (164, .043, 7), 7B py f_i+scaffold (34, .029, 1), 7B ts bug_fix (59, .034, 2), 7B ts f_i (164, .043, 7), 7B ts f_i+scaffold (34, .029, 1). Arm-level rows (3.5-5.8pp) exist but are labelled 'not the bench's resolution' and ADR-0026 forbids quoting them pooled.
- What 257 -> 400 problems buys, per stratum, under measured psi (computed with tools/power/mde.py, stratum sizes scaled 59->92, 164->255, 34->53): the one resolving stratum improves 8.5pp -> 6.7pp (1.27x — the ~1.3x claim is CONFIRMED). Five strata newly resolve: 1.5B py f_i, 7B py f_i, 7B ts f_i all reach 3.9pp (psi .043); 1.5B py bug_fix reaches 9.8pp and 7B py bug_fix 10.9pp — but those two are marginal, detectable only within ~0.4-1.0pp of psi itself. Six strata stay unresolvable at 400: all four function_implementation+scaffold cells (need ~272 cells, have 53) and bench-ts bug_fix at both tiers (need 154/232, have 92). No stratum is unresolvable at literally any n — required_n at delta=psi is 58-272 cells — so 'seven strata unresolvable at any n' (identity-surface section 4, ADR-0019 amendment) should be restated as 'six at 400-problem scaling, two more only marginally resolvable'.
- Denominator ambiguity the sizing section must resolve before quoting any 400-problem figure: '400' as 400 swept problems per arm (my scaling above, and responsiveness.py's 800-cell table) vs 400 AUTHORED problems of which only the bench half (~53.2%, 213 problems, 426 cells) is ever swept (ADR-0021's second 2026-08-12 amendment, implemented in redundancy.py). ADR-0021's open question 1: 400 bench-half problems means ~790 authored. The 8.2pp vs 11.3pp pair is exactly this ambiguity at psi_draw=0.659.
- psi_draw = 0.659 must not be used for sizing: redundancy.py:74-78 and responsiveness.py's docstring both state it is NOT psi and bounds nothing; #272 additionally showed that under Gate.run pinned-pass falls to zero in every stratum, collapsing psi_draw into the pass rate (while still running 2.2-6.0x headroom, so it measures something unnamed). The only measured per-lever psi values on the record are the norule ones above (0.029-0.134); ADR-0019's planning prior was 0.10-0.35. Sizing at psi=0.10-0.45 vs psi_draw spans 4.5pp-11.3pp at 400 authored (ADR-0021's table, reproduced) — a 2.5x range on one unmeasured number.
- The adoption-bar: CONFIRMED none is set. ADR-0019 as amended 2026-08-17 (#299): 'no bar was ever set' — b is a free parameter, the three 5pp appearances are a quantum, a table row, and an outcome, none a target; D3 makes the rule per lever class. ADR-0035 (#301) bans the bare word 'bar'. What the plan must therefore DO before sizing any campaign: obtain an owner decision per lever class it will size for — Class R (prompt/caps/granularity/breadth-default) needs no number, its adoption-bar IS the bench's MDE, so sizing the bench is itself the Class R decision; Class O (decomposition, attempts>1, breadth>1) needs the owner to state the exchange rate (gain vs per-task cost) — none is recorded anywhere; Class W (fine-tuning, #221) needs an owner-decided b plus second-tier replication and backend-survival (ADR-0018 Q4, #189's 2.6pp backend swing). D2's fitness test 'MDE <= b and d < b, per tier, in writing, before an arm runs' is uncomputable for O and W until those decisions exist. Additionally ADR-0021's amendment rules: 'Nothing about the campaign's size should be decided before #231 reports' the real per-lever psi.
- Blocking structure for the plan: #271 (OPEN) blocks the sizing of every future arm — the wall (6 vs 5) is a property of the chosen test (exact conditional vs mid-p, Fagerland 2013), ADR-0019 is not yet amended (no 'mid-p' anywhere in docs/decisions), and the fix requires one shared significance implementation where three constants and two implementations exist today; adopting mid-p flips no committed verdict, so it can be decided on paper without rig time. #272 (OPEN) blocks #224's S1/S2 sweeps (6.4-11.4h rig time, scoped for a superseded premise) AND the 257->400 decision itself — the case for growing turns on whether new material is more responsive than the current corpus, which is #224's unmeasured responsive fraction 'gated by #272' (identity-surface section 4); its cheap first test (does a pre-gate formatting pass restore pinned-pass mass?) is offline on candidates already on disk. #263 (OPEN) blocks any figure its three tools publish: item 1 (greedy+sampled pooling in ablation_report.counts, tools/bench/ablation_report.py:165-184) is confirmed and unfixed; item 2 (Pearson CI ~12x narrow, redundancy.py:245) is now confirmed by re-derivation; item 3 (11.3 vs 8.2pp) is explained — different denominators, responsiveness.py's 800 superseded — and is a cheap doc/code fix.
- D9/BOUND_MATCH for the plan: enforcement is owned by #231 and costs ~zero (#289: a subset of an already-paired set is already paired, so a subset bound is a recomputation over verdicts on disk), but whoever closes it must FIRST decide one of two invariant changes: either the bound key stops being a subset of the run key (breaking tests/test_bench_identity.py:260, set(BOUND_MATCH) <= set(KEY), since 'cells' is in neither KEY nor RECORDED — a run manifest does not carry it), or 'cells' becomes a recorded field of the run. Promoting 'cells' also requires dropping it from BOUND_MATCH_PENDING in the same change (tests/test_bench_ratecard.py:210). Until enforced, every reproducibility bound silently transfers to subsets it never saw: the 1.47pp bound measured over 257 cells is ~7x too strict on a 34-cell eligible set (Wilson upper(0,34) = 10.15pp).
- Numbers the plan may quote only with caveats: everything from tools/bench/resolution.py (untested, label-keyed on tier/arm rather than content signatures, and its 1.5B stock run carries no round pin); every ablation m/p (computed over pooled greedy+sampled draws, #263 item 1); any psi_draw (not psi, and collapsed under the gate bar per #272).
