### Q: Q2: is #269's 'the null cannot distinguish thin acceptance from a precise instrument' a commissioning gap (a check that cannot fail) or out of #231's scope as defined?

### Q: Q3: what would closing #258 change in practice, and is the honest fix a canary per rung or redefining the declared bar to what CAN fire?
VERDICT: CORRECTED: closing it changes zero published numbers and neither proposed fix is honest alone — the right fix is #258's own hybrid. Practical effect of closing: scope/secrets/structured fired 0 times in 514 rows, so they rejected nothing; every measured rate is unchanged. What changes is meaning: manifests can distinguish 'this rung passed' from 'this rung could not fire' (the ruff-with-no-config failure mode, currently living in the detector itself), and require_rungs' docstring stops being false. 'Canary per rung' is impossible for structured — it matches .json/.yaml/.yml and all 514 contracts allow only solution.py/solution.ts, so it is structurally unreachable, and a canary would require a corpus change, not a control. 'Redefine the declared bar to what can fire' has a hidden cost the question underweights: gate_rungs is a key field in reproducibility.json's bound matching ('a bar that scores differently produces a different null, which is why gate_rungs is in the key'), so shrinking GATE_RUNGS re-keys and formally voids all four declared bounds. The honest fix: canaries for the reachable-but-silent rungs (scope and secrets are observationally silent, not structurally unreachable — a synthetic candidate touching a forbidden path or embedding a credential can fire them; adapters already has lint+format; acceptance fires constantly), plus a recorded 'structurally unreachable on this corpus' entry for structured, plus the manifest distinction. That keeps GATE_RUNGS stable so no bound is re-keyed, and it is entirely offline — zero rig time. Since #258 declares 'Blocks: #231's commissioning, which rests on this control', it belongs on the r2 list.
EVIDENCE: gh issue view 258 body: 'structured provably cannot — it matches .json/.yaml/.yml and every one of the 514 contracts targets solution.py or solution.ts'; tools/bench/score.py:53 GATE_RUNGS, :355 CANARY_EXPECTS = ('lint','format') both languages; reproducibility.json 'matching': bound keys on (model, tier, gate_rungs, serving_build, cells); #258 header: 'Blocks: #231's commissioning'

## new_findings
- The control's stock arm IS the null pair's run A (control.py:70-72), so at r2 a positive control costs only one extra norule pass per cell (~17.7 min at 1.5B-ts), not a fresh pair — the r1 design already amortizes this and no phase-1 reader had priced it.
- Realized (not just ceiling) false-pass inflation in the committed null runs is at most 1/257 = 0.39pp per bench-py arm: of the three weak-acceptance tasks, only b430-sort-by-len passed (True at both tiers in bench-null-gate-15b-a and 7b-a results.jsonl); b322-fit-budget and b348-bill-split failed at both tiers — and b430's pass may even be a genuinely correct solution, since the ceiling assumes the model wrote the wrong one.
- The bench-ts@1.5B control verdict is arithmetically robust to the corpus defects: worst-case deletion of all 8 defective tasks from the majority side of the m=40 (34/6) discordant split leaves 26/6, exact p = 5.4e-4 — still far past any threshold. The corpus findings cannot un-pass check 2's one passing cell.
- gate_rungs is a key field of reproducibility.json's bound matching, so #258's 'redefine the declared bar to what can fire' option would formally void all four declared bounds — a cost neither #258 nor the phase-1 map states. The canary-plus-unreachability-record route keeps the key stable.
- Scope and secrets are observationally silent (0 firings in 514 rows) but structurally reachable — only structured is provably unreachable on this corpus. So 'canary per rung' fails for exactly one rung, and 'unreachable, recorded' is the legitimate answer #258 already provides for it.
- The 2026-08-17 comment pair on #231 reverses its own cross-arm reading same-day: lintless.py's 2x2x2 completion first concluded 'the language claim survives', then the owner's correction withdrew it (ADR-0023 clause 4: 104/257 py checkers accept only ValueError vs ts assert.throws(fn, Error)), leaving bench-py and bench-ts as 'two instruments', not a language axis. Any r2 plan quoting a ts-vs-py contrast inherits that confound at the acceptance rung itself.
- The 7B norule control is not worth re-running at r2: the effect is measured-absent at that tier (pooled 118/514 vs 119/514, p=1.00, with the mechanism table showing the adapter-rung effect collapsing from +124 lint findings to +1 between tiers). The open item is choosing a NEW effect a 7B can still be missing (#266 closed the eligibility arithmetic: capacity levers reach only 34 of 257 cells per arm).
