## The two patterns

**Pattern 1 — the instrument measures the right thing and loses it at the moment
of writing it down.** Six independent instances: `start_seconds` computed and its
return value never assigned; `repeat_spread` computed and not named by the row
builder; `declared_slots` readable from `/props` and null in every ollama ramp
row; the phase duration printed to a log and not a journal; two card readings
with only the flattering one kept; a probe-set field answered host-side and
dropped when columns were chosen. **None is "we failed to measure."**

And the guard built to prevent it is the same bug one level up: `launch.py`'s
MARKERS table asserts a *source file contains a string*. It confirms the
thermometer was installed and cannot notice that nobody wrote the temperature
down. Its entry `'"repeats": attempts'` passed throughout the campaign that
discarded `repeats`.

**Pattern 2 — findings arrive faster than the issue structure can hold them.**
29 findings in one session; #322 alone would swallow 13. Diagnosed by the owner
as three things at once: **drain rate**, **placement has no rule**, and **volume
per container**. Not visibility.

**The proposal on the table**, accepted in principle but not yet written down as

mechanically verifies*. A finding becomes a check rather than a paragraph;

the append-only record names its check, binding prose to predicate.

**Owner's ruling: prove it on bucket A first, no ADR yet.** That is what the rest
of this session did.
