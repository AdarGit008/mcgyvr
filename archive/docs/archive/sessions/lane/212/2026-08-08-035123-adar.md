## Two things worth the next lane's attention

**A negative result is in the record on purpose.** Size-standardising the gap
gives +16.6pp against a crude +18.9pp, but the 95% bootstrap interval is
[−5.1, +31.9] — it spans zero, because the 0–35-line stratum holds 5 of the 80
and carries 42% of the weight. That estimate settles nothing and the README
says so in those words. It is kept because it is the reason the sweep was worth
its hour: a lane that had stopped at the point estimate would have published a
difficulty claim its own data does not support.

**Tests were mutation-checked, and the first attempt was vacuous.** The
standardisation test originally used a fixture with identical per-stratum
rates, which passes whether or not the code weights the strata at all —
verified by reverting the weighting and watching it stay green. Rewritten with
hand-computable numbers where the weighted answer (0.2), the evenly-averaged
answer (0.1) and the raw difference (0.4) are all distinct. Both load-bearing
tests now fail under the mutation they exist to catch.
