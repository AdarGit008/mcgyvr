## new_findings
- #225's and #231's 2026-08-14 amendments both misquote the 1.5B recovery: 'bench-ts ... m = 40 (34/6)' is actually bench-ts m = 26 (2/24); m = 40 (5/35) is the pooled figure. p = 1.05e-5 and the verdict are unaffected. Re-derived via uv run --no-sync python tools/bench/control.py on the pinned runs.
- The 'no result quotable from an uncommissioned cell' doctrine — the load-bearing rule of the whole per-tier commissioning position — has zero machine enforcement. It exists only in #231/#225 amendment prose, which is precisely the decay mode #302 was filed about (ADRs written but not enforced in code).
- The open decision is framed too narrowly in #231's closing line ('a positive control that survives above the floor unit'): bench-py at the FLOOR tier is also uncommissioned (norule p = 0.057 full bar, p = 0.856 correctness-only), and ADR-0021 makes the floor unit the bench's obligation. Any chosen control should be required to recover on all four tier x arm cells, or the floor itself stays half-dark.
- The survey's headroom ceilings (73/58 at 7B) are measured on the norule pair and are not transferable to a different lever as-is; the lever-independent lower bound is the stock pass count (67/51 at 7B, 23/33 at 1.5B from the committed comparator runs) — every candidate eligible on all 257 cells clears the m >= 6 wall on all four cells either way.
- All four 1.5B commissioning runs predate the round pin — eligibility.py prints 'round: none recorded — it cannot be laid beside an arm measured in a round' for the 2026-08-13 runs. A new-lever campaign at both tiers inside the new (drained) round cures this as a side effect: it gives the floor tier its first round-stamped stock + control pair.
- Rig-time prices from tools/bench/rate-card.json (257 cells, setup 1.67 min/pass): one pass costs 16.4 min (7B py), 26.0 (7B ts), 9.8 (1.5B py), 17.7 (1.5B ts). A fresh stock+ablated contrast: ~85 min for both 7B arms, ~55 min for both 1.5B arms, ~140 min (~2h20m) for all four cells. Fresh stock passes are unavoidable because the new lever moves the product digest and old-round runs cannot be comparators (report.COMPARABLE refuses tables spanning two revisions). Caveat from the card itself: rates predate #261/PR #292's linter-crash fix; re-derive rather than transfer across that boundary.
- anonymise (#267) as commissioning control costs ~2.5x more rig time under its own revised acceptance (~5 conditions: stock + random-string + style negative control + two neutral variants = ~212 min at the 7B alone, ~350 min both tiers) plus the largest build of any candidate (four-site rename incl. staged accept.py — unreachable by ablate() today — with renamed-reference self-check). Its generation-task prior art is the strongest (RADAR: name->foo, -5.6 to -17.2% on <=3B models; ReCode 3.4: placeholder 1.5-16.7%, random string 9-55%, ordered monotonically), but ReCode's largest relative drop was on its smallest model, so 7B decay is not excluded, and #270's over-trust hypothesis predicts a possible sign FLIP on the 59-cell bug_fix stratum.
- nostop is disqualified as the commissioning control by its own prior: a worker that ignores stop conditions behaves the same as one never told, so the ablation is plausibly a no-op — a control should be an effect we expect to find (survey, and confirmed same 257-cell eligibility). Keep it as an ADR-0018 Q1 arm.
- nolocate (bug_fix-restricted) has real 7B headroom (ceiling 36/31) but requires non-mechanical prose surgery across 118 contracts, and editing authored prose moves tasks_sha256, changing the corpus identity out from under every committed run. Survey: 'Costed here, not recommended from here.'
- #246 stays excluded as a control on both records: its +13.7pp is a bar effect expressed through the adapter rungs — exactly the mechanism the tier ladder showed decaying (norule rung table: lint +43/+124 at 1.5B collapsing to +11/+1 at 7B).

## plan_input
- RECOMMENDED ROUTE: nointerface as the commissioning control, run as one campaign at both tiers x both arms (~2h20m rig time, all four cells) inside a single drained round boundary. Build cost is the smallest possible: a matrix.json lever entry (new slot interface_section) plus one strip branch in tools/bench/matrix.py apply_message mirroring strip_output_section (~15 lines + tests); no runner changes needed. Pre-register the mechanism signature before any draw: the effect must land at the acceptance rung (wrong signature = harness cannot call the function), and an effect expressed in the adapter rungs instead is the norule mechanism again and does not count as recovery. Pre-register the known risk of a 7B null: 99.2% of contracts' prose names the function (tools/bench/prose.py measurement, quoted in #267), so a 7B may infer signatures from prose; INTERFACE also carries parameter/return shape that prose does not, which is the reason to expect a residual effect.
- RUNNER-UP: anonymise (#267), the fallback if nointerface nulls above the floor. Tradeoff stated: strongest published prior of any candidate for a generation-task effect surviving capability (RADAR name->foo; ReCode random-string 9-55%, monotone ordering), hence highest recovery probability at the 7B — against the largest build (four-site rename including the staged accept.py the ablation machinery cannot reach today, renamed-reference self-check, style negative control) and ~2.5x the rig time (~3.5h at the 7B alone under #267's revised acceptance). If invoked, run the random-string condition as headline (not function_A, the weakest published rung) with the style negative control beside it, and pre-register the bug_fix stratum's possible sign flip (#270).
- DOCTRINE for the plan of record: adopt per-cell (tier x arm) commissioning explicitly — it is already the operative rule, applied in writing twice by owner-approved #231 amendments — and require the chosen control to recover on ALL FOUR cells, because bench-py@1.5B (the floor, ADR-0021's binding obligation) is as uncommissioned as the 7B. '#302 DoD requires a control above the floor' is the wrong frame; the honest frame is 'every cell a comparison will be quoted from needs a recovered control, and today that is 3 of 4 cells.'
- REQUIRED HARDENING (belongs in the same plan item): a commissioning registry as data — e.g. tools/bench/commissioning.json keyed (model, tier/arm), listing which cells have a recovered control and citing the runs — read by every figure-producing tool the same way mode.py and product.py banners are, with a test that discovers figure tools rather than naming them (the check-6 pattern). Without it the quotability rule lives only in issue prose, which is the decay #302 exists to stop.
- ADR MAINTENANCE: ADR-0018 Q3 needs an amendment either way — it still names CLM-0017's '~+20pp output-shape line' as the known effect after that attribution was corrected (#225, 2026-08-10) and after the control measured null on 3 of 4 cells (2026-08-14). The amendment should state the per-cell commissioning rule so doctrine and issue record agree.
- ERRATum to file: correct '#225/#231 2026-08-14 amendments: bench-ts@1.5B m = 40 (34/6)' to 'm = 26 (2/24); m = 40 (5/35) is the pooled figure' — re-derivable with uv run --no-sync python tools/bench/control.py.
- SEQUENCING FACTS: adding the lever moves the product pin (matrix.json/matrix.py are in product.SURFACE), so the round boundary must be drained per ADR-0032 — batch it with every other pending identity change (e.g. #261's crashed-linter-is-a-refusal fix at PR #292) rather than opening a round for the lever alone. The rate card warns its prices do not transfer across the #261 gate change; the fresh stock passes the new round forces are already counted in the ~140 min figure and double as the floor tier's first round-stamped comparator (the 2026-08-13 1.5B runs carry no round).
