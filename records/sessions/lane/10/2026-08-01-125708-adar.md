The design is three ordered stages: collapse equal-quality ties (faster wins), then dominance (beaten on quality at equal-or-less VRAM), then measurable separation. Two real bugs found by looking at the actual ladders rather than only at green tests. Speed now breaks ties between equals only and never buys a rung. Also added Model.best_throughput filtered to the model's own backend, because the MoE's ollama figure is a different quant (CAV-02).
