## Two checks were rewritten by their own ruling

A ruling that makes a check unpassable is not honoured by leaving the check
standing. K7 asserted geometry **equality** and the ruling permits recorded
difference; K8 asserted a floor of two models per engine and the ruling
abolishes the quantity. Both were rewritten in the ruling's commit, and both are
still red — for the reason the ruling creates rather than the one it removed.

**K8's first draft was vacuously green and that is the session's sharpest
result.** It ended in `assert cells` over `directory.glob("*/run.json")`, so a
single unrelated cell directory anywhere in the campaign turned it green while
every cross-host pair it named still had zero cells. Under `strict=True` that is
an XPASS, which fails the suite, which gets "fixed" by removing the marker —
the finding closed without being fixed. It is exactly the MARKERS-table defect
ADR-0037 exists to prevent, written by the person quoting ADR-0037 in the
docstring above it. The replacement matches on the `host` and `model` inside
each cell's own `run.json` and was demonstrated: red on the frozen campaign
(4 of 4 readings uncelled), still red under that same counter-example, 3 of 4
with one cell present, green only with all four.

## The review found eighteen defects and they were fixed

An adversarial pass over the rulings commit, four lenses with each finding sent
to an independent refuter, raised 37 and 18 survived. All 18 are fixed. The ones
that mattered beyond K8's vacuity:

- **A sentence that would have reversed ADR-0038.** The K8 entry read *"a
  comparison is built on one machine"*. ADR-0038 **D2**, Accepted the same day,
  says a cross-machine question authorises its own run and gives *"which machine
  serves the 1.5B faster"* as its example; **D1** withdrew the rigs' roles. The
  ruling denies cross-host *equivalence*, not cross-machine *comparison*. Both
  the check and the record now say so, and the record says why the first draft
  over-read it.
- **K6 is not code owed.** The entry said the ramp sink does not emit
  `serving_build`. `calibrate.emit()` has merged the identity block into every
  hosted row since #326; the 2026-08-19 journals are simply older than the sink.
  #336 was corrected before any work started so nobody ticks a satisfied box.
- **The correction paragraph needed correcting.** It quoted "ramp journals
  1.09–2.27, survey 1.02–1.10" — both srv2-only subsets presented as whole
  populations, which is the exact defect the paragraph was written to name. It
  now carries a table with `n` and range per population: survey srv2 11 rows
  1.02–1.10, survey srv1 6 rows 1.03–1.59, ramp srv1 27 rows 1.00–4.16, ramp
  srv2 9 rows 1.02–15.42.
- **"Byte-identical across twelve models"** — no population of twelve exists (17
  slots, 11 models over 19 children), and only the digest's sampler *half* is
  identical, not the digest.
- Three wrong line citations in #336: `contract.py:300`→`:438`,
  `ollama.py:200`→`:211`, `ollama.py:741-742`→`:742-743`; and `ollama.py:376-380`
