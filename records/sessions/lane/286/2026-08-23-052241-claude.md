# Session — lane/286 — 2026-08-23 (twenty-first)

**The campaign was cherry-picking at the wrong time, and the owner said so.**
Multi-model residency — across models, across families, across engines — is
**bench hardening**, and hardening is post-bench and post-trunk. What stays is
whatever can be tested quickly now, which turns out to be exactly phase 0. The
rest is parked with its evidence intact.

Then the second half of the instruction, which is the standing one and is where
the rest of this session went: *every GPU run gathers all the data it can,
verified and trustworthy; a missing field is an error; a wrong field name or
role is an error; the bench is deterministic and its results reproduce.* Three
gaps were found against it and filed as **#348, #349, #350**.

## The recollection did not match the record, and the difference mattered

The session opened with "I think we decided no cross engine co-residency."
Searched: the campaign header, ADR-0038/0039/0040, the run contract, and all ten
of this lane's session records. **Nothing carries that decision and three things
carry its opposite** — `records/headers/2026-08-22-coresidency-matrix.json`'s
own question says *"across engines"*, its `prerequisites` name #343/#344/#345/#346
as "the cross-engine harness changes, for every mixed-engine cell in phases 1, 3
and 4", and the run contract §7 says cross-engine co-residency was demonstrated
by hand on both rigs. The header was rewritten at the owner's instruction on
2026-08-22 and kept it.

What *had* been decided and deferred is **throughput**, in the header's
`left_on_table`, "deliberately and in full". That is the nearby decision, and it
is not this one.

**Saying so was worth more than agreeing.** Ruling out cross-engine alone would
have retired #343 and #346 and left #344 and #347 standing, because neither is a
cross-engine defect: **#344**'s `card_idle_before_load is True` gate fires on a
second *ollama* model loading onto a card its sibling already holds, and
**#347**'s utilization precondition fires on a second *vLLM* instance on a second
port — phase 2's multiplicity arm, where the engines never mix. The ruling that
actually retires all four is the one the owner gave: park **multi-model**, not
cross-engine.

## The audit: three gaps against the standing requirement

### #348 — the scored path never reads the card

Every reading that describes the machine exists as a declared constant, and the
instrument that produces the trunk's numbers calls none of them.
`contract.snapshot` (`contract.py:512`) has **one** production caller,
`serving/run.py:287` — the *calibration* runner. `CARD_STATE_COMMAND`
(`contract.py:603`: temperature, power, SM clock, throttle mask) is read by the
calibration ramp (`contract.py:697`) and by a vLLM claim (`vllm.py:620`).
Neither runs on a scored sweep.

What a sweep does capture is `pin.host_block` → `{engine, instance, machine,
fingerprint}`, which is rich about the **server's configuration** and holds no
reading of the **card** at all. A sweep that throttles for an hour records
slower `latency_s` and nothing that says why.

**It is the run contract's own §3 pathology one level out.** That section warns
that readings keep being minted and never wired, and names three. This is the
same shape at the level of the instrument, and §3's principle — record the
unrecoverable — is stated in the document and unimplemented in the rig.

Second half: the capture happens **twice, at the two edges** (`AT_OPEN`
`breadth/measure.py:1236`, `AT_CLOSE` `:1779`). Anything that starts after open
and ends before close is invisible, and the two captures agree while it is.

### #349 — an empty host block means four different things

`_host_block` ends `except Exception: return {}`
(`breadth/measure.py:253`, and byte-identical in `bundle/measure.py:234`), and
`pin.host_block` returns `{}` for a loopback endpoint (`pin.py:216`) and for a
host that answered nothing (`pin.py:219`). So *"there is no machine to log
into"*, *"the endpoint is loopback"*, *"ssh failed"* and *"pin.py raised"* all
arrive as the same value.

**This is the collapse the tree refuses in two other places on purpose** —
`gpu_idle`'s "`None` is NOT idle" (`contract.py:538-544`) and the compute-apps
probe's `&&`-not-`;` sentinel (`contract.py:478`), both of which exist so that an
unread card cannot parse as an empty one. Ninth instance on this lane of the
shape: here, a capture that succeeds because its failure path and its empty path
are the same value.

### #350 — `concurrency` is refused in one half of the record and captured in the other

`observed.json` is `{native: …, host: …}`. The **native** block refuses
`concurrency` on both engines (`observed.py:781`, `:959`), and both refusals are
correct about the network surface and exhaustively searched — the ollama one
says in its own words that the answer is *"obtainable only with access to the
serving host (`tools/bench/serving/`), never from here."*

**The `host` block beside it is produced by that module, with that access, and
already carries the number.** `serving_config` parses `-np` → `n_parallel`
(`ollama.py:770`) and lifts `total_slots` off `/props` (`ollama.py:757`), and
both survive as readable material (`fingerprint.py:350-354`). The tree has even
classified them: `fingerprint.py:160-163` files both in the **semantic** half,
under the comment *"batching and caching — decide whether a re-run reproduces at
all."* The field declared for exactly that purpose reads `null` beside them.

**And the client half is a constant nobody writes down.** Determinism is a
property of the *realised* batch; the server's width is only a ceiling. This
bench dispatches one request at a time — `max_parallel=1` hard-coded at
`bundle/measure.py:538`, used by breadth through the same worker
(`breadth/measure.py:1665`), with `_draws` a plain sequential loop. That is a
fact the harness owns outright, and it is written nowhere.

**So reproducibility is available and is not claimed.** ADR-0027's consequence —
greedy is not deterministic under continuous batching — is the correct reading of
vLLM #23138, and the same result's other half is that a *single client* was
deterministic over 70+ rounds. The property is not missing. The evidence that
its conditions held is.

## What the requirement already has, so it is not starting from zero

Worth stating, because three findings read like an indictment and the tree is
better than that. *A missing field is an error* has real mechanisms:
`test_every_nested_key_of_checks_has_a_disposition` (a new key needs a sink that
says what became of it — it refused #345 last session), ADR-0027 D2's three
states, and `PENDING_REASON`, which is why the four probe-set fields were
traceable at all. *A wrong field name or role is an error* has
`DECLARED_DUPLICATES` in `tests/test_four_lenses.py:85` (a constant name in two
modules must be declared and the declarations must agree),
`test_a_backend_never_names_another_backend`, and `headers.py`'s closed `KEYS`.
The **model and engine** side of capture is genuinely exhaustive — `observed.py`
discovered vLLM's route table from `/openapi.json` rather than from docs and
takes every parameterless GET.

**The gap is the machine side of the scored path**, and all three findings are
on it.
