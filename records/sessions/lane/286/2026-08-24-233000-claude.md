## Four corrections, and the discipline they share

1. **The srv1 control bar was set against the n=256 maximum** (295), not
   yesterday's n=32 reading (229-231). The runner read 229.7, declared the rig
   changed, and skipped every remaining srv1 block by rule. The rig had not
   changed; the skipped cells were re-run with `--only`, the skipped rows kept.
2. **LMDeploy died in 8 s and `--rm` erased its log** — #352's mechanism, live
   again in a runner written the same day. Reproduced by hand without `--rm`:
   `IncompleteSnapshotError` under `HF_HUB_OFFLINE=1`, because vLLM's download
   skips `.gitattributes`, `LICENSE`, `README.md` and `huggingface_hub` calls
   that incomplete. Fixed by handing LMDeploy the local snapshot directory with
   `--model-name` keeping the hub id; `--rm` dropped so a dying container keeps
   its log.
3. First believed srv1-only, because srv2's 1.5B launched on the old code.
4. Then srv2's 7B and 3B snapshots turned out incomplete too; four srv2 cells
   plus the re-take were appended on the corrected launch.

**Run the launch by hand before believing the runner's refusal.** Every one of
the four was found that way, and the second would have been invisible without
it.

## Kept

- **A bar derived from the wrong level is a false alarm with teeth.** The
  runner did exactly what the spec said and stopped a healthy rig. The rule was
  right; its number was copied from the wrong row.
- **A refusal with no log is not a refusal.** `--rm` on a launch that can fail
  is the #352 defect in a new coat, and it was written knowing #352.
- **The rate is comparable; the work is not.** ollama and LMDeploy cannot force
  475 tokens (no `ignore_eos`): every request stopped at EOS at 323 tokens and
  `cap_fraction` reads 0.0 for both engines. Tokens over wall is still a rate,
  and the re-take filter (cap >= 0.9) excludes those engines by construction.
  The vLLM controls ran through the repo's own `sweep.py` (its prompt, mean
  latency). B2-3 was skipped by its own rule. One day, one pass except the
  re-takes.
