# Context Size vs Quality — Results & Verdict

**Issue:** #27 · **Lane:** lane/27 · **Run:** 2026-07-28, 15:40–16:07 UTC
**Design:** `research/context_size_experiment_2026-07-28.md`
**Raw data:** `results_q3b.jsonl`, `results_qwen3.jsonl` (160 runs: 2 models ×
4 conditions × 20 tasks, greedy, one remediation round)

## Headline

**The strict-minimal-context assumption is wrong for the small worker.**
A ~2 KB skill bundle made qwen2.5-coder:3b both markedly more correct
(**45% → 70% first-pass acceptance**) and ~2.5× faster (output discipline
stops rambling). Quality falls off again at 8 KB. The 30B MoE is at ceiling
on this task set and is insensitive to bundle size.

## Results

|-----------|:------:|:-----:|:---------:|:----:|:----:|

## Findings

1. **Break-even is immediate for the 3b, peak at ~2 KB.** Even 0.4 KB of
   role + output rules is worth +15 pp. The 2 KB bundle (standards +
   edge-case checklist + pitfalls) adds another +10 pp. The 8 KB handbook
   gives back 10 pp versus c2 — the falloff the counter-hypothesis
   predicted, arriving somewhere between 2 KB and 8 KB.
2. **Bundles make the small model FASTER, not slower.** Under c0 the 3b
   averages 403 completion tokens (explanations, repeated attempts); output
   rules cut that to ~100–125. Completion tokens dominate wall time, so c2
   is ~2.5× faster than c0 despite a 3× larger prompt. Token counts prove
   this independently of any machine-load noise.
3. **What the bundle rescues (3b):** algorithmic and bug-fix tasks —
   flatten, topo sort, binary search, mutable-default-arg, config parser
   all flip from fail to pass by c2.
4. **What no bundle rescues:** subtle contract constraints. Both models
   mutate the input via inner-list aliasing on t02 (merge_intervals) under
   every condition; the 3b never honors "bool is not numeric" (t19) and
   never uses the pinned modern annotation form (t17/t18, emits
   `typing.List[str]`). Contract-compliance details need a verifier (#20),
   not more context.
5. **Remediation is nearly worthless at the worker tier:** 2 rescues in 35
   remediation attempts across both models. Failed local output should
   escalate (feeds #24's escalation rules), not retry locally.
6. **30B: bundles are quality-neutral, latency-positive at small sizes**
   (c2 ~25% faster than c0 via shorter outputs; c3's 2 K prompt tokens cost
   ~5 s of prefill at partial offload and cancel the win).

## Caveats

- n=20, single greedy seed: per-condition differences of ±1 task (5 pp) are
  noise. The 3b's c0→c2 delta is +5 tasks and the direction agrees with the
  latency/token mechanism, so it is treated as signal; the 30B's ±1
  wobbles are not.
- t17/t18 acceptance requires the modern `list[str]` form (the contract
  pins it, and `typing.List[str] != list[str]` on Python 3.14). Both fail
  uniformly across all 3b conditions, so the curve shape is unaffected;
  absolute 3b rates are ~10 pp understated if you consider the legacy form
  acceptable.
- Another interactive session was active on the box during the run:
  latency means carry contention noise (token counts do not).
- Q2_K quantization on the 30B may behave differently at higher quants.

## Verdict

**Adopt minimal + compact skill bundle, capped at ~2 KB, for local
workers.** Keep contracts as the user message; ship the c2 bundle
(`data/context_exp/bundles/c2.md`) as the worker system prompt. Do not
scale bundles with more "helpful" context — 8 KB measurably degrades the
small model and buys nothing on the large one.

Consequences for open work:

- The lane/16 hard constraint "no change may inflate worker prompts"
  should be amended to "worker prompts = contract + capped ≤2 KB skill
  bundle" (this experiment is the data that sanctions the change).
- The skill-spike decision deferred in #23: proceed, with the 2 KB cap.
- #24 (harden cascade): use bundle c2 as the default worker system prompt;
  escalation on first acceptance failure, no local retry (finding 5).
- #20 (verifier): prioritize contract-compliance checks (mutation,
  type-form, forbidden constructs) — the failure class context cannot fix.
